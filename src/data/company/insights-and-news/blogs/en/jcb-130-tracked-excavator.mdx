## Best Practices for Foundation and Basement Excavation

To maximize the performance and safety of your JCB 130 tracked excavator during foundation and basement work, follow these industry best practices:

### Pre-Excavation Planning
- **Site Survey**: Conduct thorough ground surveys to identify soil types, water table levels, and underground utilities before deploying the JCB 130 tracked excavator.
- **Access Planning**: Ensure adequate ramp angles (maximum 1:6 gradient) for the JCB 130 tracked excavator to enter and exit basement excavations safely.
- **Spoil Management**: Plan spoil placement or truck loading zones within the JCB 130 tracked excavator optimal reach radius (7-8 meters) to minimize cycle times.
- **Shoring Requirements**: For basement depths exceeding 1.5 meters in loose soil, install appropriate shoring before the JCB 130 tracked excavator begins vertical wall excavation.

### During Excavation
- **Layer-by-Layer Approach**: Excavate basement pits in 1.5-2 meter lifts, allowing the JCB 130 tracked excavator to maintain stability and precision.
- **Benching**: Create benches or steps around the perimeter when working at depth, providing safe working platforms and reducing wall pressure.
- **Continuous Inspection**: Monitor excavation walls for signs of instability, cracking, or water seepage throughout the JCB 130 tracked excavator operation.
- **Proper Bucket Selection**: Use toothed buckets for breaking ground and smooth-edged buckets for final grading and foundation bottom preparation with the JCB 130 tracked excavator.
